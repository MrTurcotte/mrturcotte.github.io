Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-02-11

Idea Writer Two (“the App”) is a free, offline note-taking application designed to provide users with a distraction-free writing experience. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to transparency.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data. All notes and information you enter into the App remain on your device and are never transmitted, shared, or accessed by us or any third parties.

2. Data Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit any data, your notes remain private and are stored locally on your device. You are responsible for securing your device and backups.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. Children’s Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, the App is safe for users of all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. However, our core principle remains: we do not collect or share your data. If any changes are made, they will be reflected in this document.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at the email address in the Google Play Store.
